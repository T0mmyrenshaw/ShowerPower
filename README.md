# Shower Power Slots

A bathroom-themed, Vegas-style slot machine that runs in the browser. It's a single HTML file with no build step and no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch) and visit the link it gives you.

## How it plays

- 6 reels × 4 rows, 4,096 ways to win. Symbols pay left to right on neighboring reels, anywhere in each column.
- Bets from $0.20 to $50 in play money. You start with $500, and the "landlord" tops you up if you run out.
- **Rubber duck** is wild on reels 2–6.
- **Full Blast** (3+ shower heads): 8 free spins, plus 2 for each extra shower head. Raindrops turn symbols into wild ducks, every win pays ×2, and 3 more shower heads add 5 spins.
- **Plumber Party** (6+ plumbers): pick bolts and a wrench unscrews them. Match 3 jackpot tokens to win MINI, MINOR, MAJOR or GRAND.
- GRAND and MAJOR are progressive and grow with every bet.
- Press SPIN while the reels turn to slam-stop them.

The game pays back about 95% over time. Full Blast hits about 1 in 340 spins, and Plumber Party about 1 in 140.

## Sound

Every screen has its own generated spa soundtrack. The music-note and speaker buttons in the top corner toggle music and sound effects.

Play money only. No real-money gambling.
